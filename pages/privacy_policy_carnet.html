1. Overview

Carnet ("the App") is a credit-book and product-list manager built for shop owners and individuals. Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and the security measures in place to protect it.

Effective date: August 23 2026  |  Version: 0.0.1

🔍

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Carnet works 100 % offline and never connects to the internet.

The only data the App creates is the content you enter:

  • Client / credit list names you create
  • Product names and prices you enter inside lists
  • App language preference (English, Français, or العربية)

All of this data is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to any server.

3. How Your Data Is Stored

Carnet uses two on-device storage mechanisms:

  • SQLite database (sqflite) – stores your credit lists and product entries inside the App's private sandbox. No other app can access this data.
  • SharedPreferences – stores your selected language preference as a simple key-value pair.

Both are protected by Android's standard app isolation. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all data from your device.

🔑

4. App Permissions

Carnet requests no special Android permissions. It does not ask for access to:

  • Camera, microphone, or contacts
  • Location (GPS or network-based)
  • Storage / media files
  • Internet / network connectivity
  • Notifications
🔗

5. Third-Party Services

Carnet integrates no third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services. The App uses only these open-source Flutter packages, all of which operate entirely on-device:

  • sqflite – local SQLite database
  • shared_preferences – lightweight local key-value store
  • provider – in-memory state management (no I/O)
  • animated_splash_screen – splash animation (UI only)
👶

6. Children's Privacy

Carnet does not knowingly collect any data from or about children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Because no data is collected at all, the App is safe for users of any age.

⚖️

7. Your Rights

Because all data resides solely on your device, you have full and direct control:

  • Access & export – your data is stored in the App's private SQLite file on your device.
  • Deletion – delete individual lists/products inside the App, or uninstall it to erase everything.
  • Portability – root-access backups or Android's "Back up my data" option may include the App's data.

Since no data is sent to us, there is no server-side data for us to delete on your behalf.
